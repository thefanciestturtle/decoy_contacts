Version 2.0.0: Decoy Contacts is now its own app, with a new icon, next to Fake Contacts 1.8.
- Delete removes only contacts this app created, never real ones with the same name.
- Create no longer makes duplicates.
- Asks first, with the exact count; big batches show progress and a Stop button.
- Plain error messages, a status line, a live preview, Reset to defaults, About.
- Dark mode, and better TalkBack and keyboard support.
- Phone numbers now use the UK range reserved for TV and film.
